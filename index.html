How it works

Five steps, and you don't have to carry a thing

  1. Book online

    Fill in the 3-minute form. You get a booking ID straight away by email.

  2. Pack up

    We drop off boxes and tape, or use your own. Write your label, like HS-014-03, on each item.

  3. We collect

    We pick everything up from your room at the end of term, on the day you chose.

  4. Stored locally

    Your stash goes into our locked steel container at Hartpury Self Storage, not a warehouse miles away.

  5. Delivered back

    Tell us your return date and new address, even if that's not until August. We bring it all back.

What to store

Most of your room fits. A few things can't come.

Happy to store

  • Boxes of books, clothes and kitchen kit
  • Suitcases and holdalls
  • Duvets, pillows and bedding bags
  • Mini fridge (defrosted and dry)
  • Bikes
  • TVs and monitors (boxed or wrapped)
  • Desk chairs and clothes rails

Please don't pack

  • Food, including tins and snacks
  • Liquids, drinks and toiletries that could leak
  • Anything flammable, like gas canisters or aerosols
  • Cash, jewellery or other valuables
  • Plants, or anything alive or perishable
  • Pianos, sofas, beds, mattresses or wardrobes
  • Lithium batteries (take e-bike and e-scooter batteries out)

Large items must weigh no more than 25 kg and fit within 180 × 80 × 80 cm, or fold or come apart to fit. Not sure about something? Ask us before you book.

Questions

Things students usually ask

I don't know my return address yet. Can I still book?

Yes. Leave the return details blank when you book and let us know by August. We'll arrange a delivery slot then.

Do I need to be in when you collect?

Ideally, yes, so you can hand everything over and we can check your items against your booking. Pick a time slot that suits you when you book.

How do I label my things?

Your confirmation email has a booking sheet with a label for every item, such as HS-014-01, HS-014-02 and so on. Write one on each box, bag or large item before we arrive.

What counts as an item?

Each box, bag, suitcase or large item counts as one. Your booking covers up to 10 items in total, and up to 2 of them can be large items like a bike, a mini fridge or a desk chair.

What if I have more than 10 items?

No problem. Extra large items are £20 each. For anything else over 10 items, put your real numbers on the booking form and we'll confirm the total before you pay.

How do I pay?

Once we've confirmed your booking, pay a 10% deposit to secure your collection slot: £30, or £27 at the early bird price. The rest is due the week before we collect, and we'll remind you of the amount. Prefer to get it done? Pay in full when you book and save 5%: £285, or £256.50 at the early bird price. We’ll email you a secure payment link with your booking confirmation.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel within 14 days of booking and we'll refund your deposit in full. After that, the deposit (10% of your price) is non-refundable and you won't pay anything more. If you paid in full, we refund everything except that 10%.

Can I change my booking?

Call, text or email us with your booking ID and we'll update it for you.
