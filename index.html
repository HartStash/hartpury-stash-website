Questions

Things students usually ask

Something else? Call, text or email and we'll help.

What counts as an item?

Each box, bag, suitcase or large item counts as one. Your booking covers up to 10 items, and up to 2 of them can be large items, like a bike, a horse saddle, a desk or a large desk chair.

Will my things get damp?

No. Your belongings are kept in a safe, dry environment in our ventilated, damp-proof storage containers, on the farm where we live and work. We're a trusted local community business, and looking after people's belongings is what we do every day.

How do I get my boxes?

Pick them up on campus at one of our box days in the weeks before move-out. We'll email you the dates and where to find us with your booking confirmation. Can't make it? Collect them from the farm at a time that suits you.

I live off campus. Can you still collect and deliver?

Yes. Collection and delivery on campus are included. For an address off campus within 10 miles of Hartpury, including Gloucester and Tewkesbury, it's £10 each way. So if we collect from halls and deliver to your new house in Gloucester, that's £10 extra.

Can you deliver my things to my home address for the summer?

No. Hartpury Stash is for students coming back to Hartpury next year. We keep your things safe over the summer and bring them back to your new term-time address when you return.

I don't know my return address yet. Can I still book?

Yes. Leave it blank when you book and tell us by August. We'll arrange a delivery slot for when you're back.

Do I need to be in when you collect?

Ideally, yes, so you can hand everything over and we can check your items against your booking. Pick a time slot that suits you when you book.

How do I pay?

Book before 1 March and it's £270 for the summer. Pay the full £270 when you book, or a £27 deposit to secure your place and £243 the week before we collect. We'll email you a secure payment link with your booking confirmation.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel within 14 days of booking for a full refund. After that, the 10% deposit is non-refundable and you won't pay anything more. If you paid in full, we refund everything except that 10%.
